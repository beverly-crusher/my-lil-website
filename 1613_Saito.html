Saito is owned by the Dextameier Philosophical Institute. The institute's campuses are distributed throughout the system, with its administrative offices and employee housing on Saito 03, the "least profound body in the system."

Saito's principle world, Saito 03, is a planetoid on the edge of the system's Oort cloud. Notably, there are two other planetoids that are more condusive to habitation in the system: Saito 02-B and Saito 02-D. Saito 02-B houses the student and graduate staff dormitories, but census takers could not find anybody who would certify that they live there. Saito 02-D is the subject of a "no-rules terraforming experiment." Due to the frequency of censuses, environmental protection is recommended to all visitors despite its UWP code.

The system has 2 non-government factions: a notable non-charismatic leader and a fringe self-perpetuating oligarchy.

The culture of the planet qualifies under index: 22, deceptive.
